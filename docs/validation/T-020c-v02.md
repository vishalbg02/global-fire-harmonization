## 2. What v0.2 is (fixed before the fit)

`ratio_v02 = ratio_v01(stratum, witness, day/night, season) x f(witness, day/night, activity bin)`. The activity is the converted activity of the witness's own counts per analysis cell and month, in the eight bins of T-020b. `f = sum(Aqua) / sum(ratio_v01 x witness)` over the stratum-months of the bin, fitted on the calibration fit periods only (S-NPP 2012-01 to 2016-12, NOAA-20 2018-04 to 2021-12), one value per witness, day/night and bin (full pooling across strata). Bins with fewer than 50 units copy the nearest bin with 50 or more. `f_sd` is the spread over 1,000 resamples of whole fit months and enters as one fully correlated error per witness, day/night and bin. phi and kappa were re-estimated the same way on the fit periods: phi 2.09 (day) and 2.23 (night) against 2.25 and 2.47; kappa 0.0081 and 0.0174 against 0.0084 and 0.0164.

## 5. What this means and what it does not

- By the rule fixed in advance, **v0.1 stays the method of the products.** Nothing in `data/products/` changed. D-030 stays PROPOSED with the result recorded; the Lead decides how to close it.
- The saturation finding of T-020b is real in the fit periods (the ratio rises with activity) but a conversion that is a function of the month's own activity does not repair the extreme months in the A1 windows. The A1 failure therefore is **not** explained or repaired by this mechanism alone. Other candidates (a different overpass time sampling the diurnal peak, instrument thresholds for very large fires, the cell-day count itself saturating in a way no per-bin factor can undo) remain **hypotheses**; none was tested here.
- Documented limitation for the app and the demo: the converted S-NPP estimate is 20 to 40% below Aqua in the peak months of very large fires (C3 to C6), the intervals do not cover that gap, and the A1 family result for method 0.1.0 is FAIL (0 PASS, 4 FAIL, 2 INCONCLUSIVE). See risk S19 in `docs/RISKS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`.

## 6. Caveats of this comparison

- [LIMITATION] The factor is fitted in sample: the T-011 ratios and the factors use the same fit months, so the fit-period numbers are optimistic; the holdout and D1 numbers are out of sample.
- [LIMITATION] Errors of different bins and of the stratum ratios are treated as independent; full pooling of f across strata (no regional level).
- [LIMITATION] Night bins above 5 cell-days have very few units (313 and 17 for S-NPP), so the night factor at the high end is thin; the factors for the two highest night bins are copies.
- [ASSUMPTION] "Day+night" in condition 1 is the day+night total `T`; the day and night parts are shown beside it. Under the stricter reading (day, night and total all no worse) v0.2 also fails (countries, day and total).
